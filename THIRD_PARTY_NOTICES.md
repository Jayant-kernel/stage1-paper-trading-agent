## Vibe-Trading

The Stage 1 public daily-history fallback and strict factor-research design were
informed by the loader-registry, Yahoo loader, causal factor testing, random
controls and out-of-sample gates in
[HKUDS/Vibe-Trading](https://github.com/HKUDS/Vibe-Trading), reviewed at commit
`a5eb30fd00d6ee71cd5099d15f57de5ae47010ff`.

Only a small project-native, research-only implementation was added here. The
Vibe-Trading application, factor zoo, LLM swarm, broker execution paths and
order-related modules were not copied or installed.
